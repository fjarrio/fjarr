| 28 | **A bulk transfer over the tunnel stalls in roughly 40 % of attempts**, at the onset of the flow, and nothing at our layer sees it: zero `dropped_queue`, `dropped_mtu`, `dropped_policy` and `dropped_no_peer` at both ends, zero refused sends, zero failed writes into either device. `ssh` and small HTTP requests over the same link never stall. Ruled out in slice 4.5c: video sharing the connection (it stalls the same without it), transfer size (64 MiB and 1 GiB alike), the MTU check, the operator's writes into its TUN device, and the 4 MiB channel watermark (lowering it to 256 KiB changed nothing and never triggered).
